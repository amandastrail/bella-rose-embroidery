Ordering

How it works

Orders are placed through my Etsy shop, so checkout is simple and secure.

  1. Choose

    Pick a sweater, beanie or design from the shop, in your size and colour.

  2. Personalize

    Send the name, wording or little details you'd like stitched.

  3. I stitch

    Your piece is embroidered by hand. Allow 2-4 weeks.

  4. Enjoy

    Your sweater is on its way, ready to be worn and loved.

Pricing and sizing details are listed on each item in my Etsy shop. I also take custom items beyond sweaters. Have an idea that isn't in the shop? Send me a message and we'll talk it through.

Meet the maker

Meet Kayleigh

I'm a Jesus-loving, homesteading wife and mama of three, and I've been married for over a decade. We live in a very rural community in British Columbia with a cat, dogs, chickens and turkeys, and maybe goats soon.

I love embroidering and making special gifts for people, and that is how this little business all got started. Every sweater is made by hand, with love, the same way I'd make one for my own kids.
